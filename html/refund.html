REFUND POLICIES

Grooming Service Refund Policy

  • Cancellations made within 24 hours without rescheduling are eligible for an 80% refund.
  • Refunds are processed within 3–4 business days.
  • Service complaints must be raised within 24–48 hours.

Vet Consultation Refund Policy

Appointments cancelled at least 1 hour before consultation are eligible for refund.

Missed appointments without cancellation are non-refundable.

Refund Policy For Pet Boarding, Pet Sitting & DayCare

  • Unsuitable Pet Hero → alternate provider or booking transfer available.
  • Last-minute cancellations within 24 hours may incur a 50% fee.
  • No-show bookings may forfeit full amount.
  • Refunds after 6 months cannot be processed.
  • Other cancellations may receive refund minus 25% handling fee.
  • Sharing personal contact details may incur 50% platform fee.
  • Instant cancellations include 15% handling fee.

Refund Policy For Adoption & Re-homing Listing Platform Fee

  • Adoption platform fee is non-refundable.
  • Re-homing listing fee is non-refundable.
  • Refunds are only provided for:
    • Duplicate payments
    • Technical failures
    • Failed payment issues
  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days.

Refund Policy For Dog Walking

  • Cancellation within 48 hours after service starts → 50% fee.
  • Last-minute cancellations → 25% fee.
  • Shared contact details → 25% platform fee.
  • Instant cancellations → 15% handling fee.

Dog Training Service Refund Policy

Refunds may apply under the following conditions:

  1. Trainer no-show without reschedule → full refund.
  2. Cancellation before first session → up to 90% refund.
  3. Partial refund for partially used services.
  4. Replacement or refund for unprofessional behaviour.

Non-Refundable Situations:

  • More than 3 sessions completed
  • Undisclosed aggressive pet behaviour
  • Missed sessions without notice
  • Late cancellations within 12 hours

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.

Partner Refund Policy

  • Onboarding fee is non-refundable
  • Wallet recharges are non-refundable
  • KYC verification is mandatory
  • Dashboard access available within 24–72 hours

These policies ensure fair platform usage and prevent misuse. For support queries, please contact customer support.
