REFUND POLICIES

Grooming Service Refund Policy

  • Cancellations made within 24 hours without rescheduling an appointment will be subject to a partial refund of 80% of the booking price.
  • Once an order is cancelled and the refund is initiated, it will take 3–4 business days for the amount to be credited to your original payment method.
  • In case of dissatisfaction with the service, we request you to share any concerns within 24–48 hours after the service. Failing to do so will make the booking ineligible for refund; however, we will assist with the best possible resolution.

Vet Consultation Refund Policy

In the event of cancellation of an appointment for a consultation at least 1 hour prior to the scheduled tele-consultation or home visit, any amount paid will be refunded to your original payment method.

If you do not cancel the appointment but fail to attend the scheduled consultation with the veterinarian, no refunds will be made.

Refund Policy For Pet Boarding, Pet Sitting & DayCare

  • Suitability of Pet Hero: If your chosen Pet Hero is deemed unsuitable after the initial conversation, we will provide an alternate Pet Hero. If you'd like to select a different Pet Hero, the amount paid will be transferred to your new booking. Any excess will be refunded to your balance for future bookings (refunds may be subject to transaction fees, if applicable).
  • Last-Minute Cancellations: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the booking may incur a 50% cancellation fee.
  • No-Show: If no cancellation request is made and you do not show up, the full booking amount may be forfeited.
  • Refund Limitations: Refunds requested more than 6 months after the transaction date cannot be processed.
  • Other Cancellations: You may request a full refund minus a 25% handling fee, or reschedule your booking to avoid cancellation fees.
  • Platform Fee: If personal information (phone numbers, emails, etc.) has been exchanged between customer and service provider, a 50% platform fee will be charged.
  • Instant Cancellations: A 15% handling fee will apply to cancellations made immediately after booking.

Refund Policy For Adoption & Re-homing Listing Platform Fee

  • Adoption Platform Fee — Non-Refundable: The adoption platform fee is non-refundable once paid, even if the adoption does not happen or is delayed.
  • Re-homing Listing Fee — Non-Refundable: The listing fee is non-refundable once the listing is created, approved, or published.
  • Refunds Only in Limited Cases:
    • Duplicate payment was made for the same transaction, or
    • Payment succeeded but the service/listing was not activated due to a verified technical issue, or
    • Amount was debited but payment shows failed in the system.
  • Processing: Eligible refund requests must be raised within 7 days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days.

Refund Policy For Dog Walking

  • Mid-Service Cancellations: If cancelled within 48 hours after service initiation, a 50% fee applies. After 48 hours, the full booking amount may be forfeited.
  • Last-Minute Cancellations: Less than 24 hours before booking may incur a 25% fee.
  • Platform Fee: If contact details are shared, a 25% platform fee applies.
  • Instant Cancellations: A 15% handling fee applies.

ThePetNest Dog Training Service Refund Policy

You are eligible for a refund under the following conditions:

  1. Trainer No-Show: If trainer fails to attend and no reschedule is made within 24 hours, a full refund is issued.
  2. Service Not Started: Cancellation before first session → up to 90% refund.
  3. Service Partially Used: Refund calculated after deducting sessions used + administrative charges.
  4. Mismatch / Unprofessional Behavior: Replacement or partial refund may be provided.

Non-Refundable Scenarios:

  • Cancellation after more than 3 sessions completed
  • Aggressive pet behavior not disclosed earlier
  • Missed sessions without notice
  • Late cancellations (within 12 hours)

Rescheduling Policy:

  • Allowed up to 12 hours before session

Refund Processing:

  • Refunds are credited within 5–7 business days

ThePetNest Partner’s Refund Policy

  • Wallet Recharges: Non-refundable and non-transferable
